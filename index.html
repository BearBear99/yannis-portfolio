About Me

Hi, I'm Hong Yiu Lo (Yannis), a design student currently studying UI/UX. I previously studied Community Service, which helps me better understand people's feelings and needs when creating designs and user experiences.

Growing up in the fast-paced and highly competitive city of Hong Kong has trained me to work efficiently and learn quickly.

My design style leans towards clean and simple, with a cute touch. I enjoy creating IP characters, logos, and posters. I also take freelance projects regularly and work on different types of design work.
